Server NAP Rules

Non-Aggression Pact for the Top 12 Alliances

The Spirit of the Pact

The Non-Aggression Pact (NAP) is in effect for the Top 12 alliances on the server. Its purpose is to foster strategic growth, diplomacy, and focused competition, rather than constant, chaotic warfare. Adherence to these rules is mandatory for all members of NAP alliances.

Rules of Engagement

  • ✔
    Diplomacy First: No base hits or resource tile hits are permitted unless previously and clearly agreed upon by the leadership of both alliances involved.
  • ✔
    Limited PvP: Small-scale, organized PvP is allowed. This encourages tactical skirmishes over server-wide chaos.

Prohibited Actions

  • ✖
    No Unapproved Aggression: Random attacks on bases, resource tile stealing, or any solo PvP without explicit prior approval from both alliances is strictly forbidden.
  • ✖
    Attack Limits: Do not attack more than 5 members per enemy alliance per day. This prevents targeted harassment.
  • ✖
    No Full-Scale War: Do not launch full alliance invasions or make attempts to "full wipe" an opponent. Coordinated mass attacks are a violation of the NAP.

Consequences for Violations

Accountability is crucial for the NAP's success. Players who break these rules will face immediate consequences to maintain fairness and order on the server.

Violators will be banned from joining any Top 12 NAP alliance for 7 days.

These rules are subject to change. Please check back regularly for updates.
